What Santa Cruz Strength is

Santa Cruz Strength is an independent strength training gym at 151 Harvey West Blvd, Suite D, in Santa Cruz, California. The floor supports barbell training, powerlifting and general strength work, with racks, bars, platforms and open gym space.

Personal training is available for lifters who want technique coaching, structured programming, or help getting started. Membership options include day passes, monthly plans and longer-term memberships.

Built for strength training

Four disciplines, one floor. The equipment is shared, the standards are not lowered for any of them.

  1. Powerlifting

    Squat, bench and deadlift training on rack and platform space, with the bars and plates a competitive total needs. The gym hosts sanctioned meets on site.

  2. Olympic weightlifting

    Snatch and clean and jerk work on lifting platforms with bumper plates, so the lifts can be dropped where they are meant to be dropped.

  3. Strongman

    One of the three disciplines this gym was built around. Implement training happens here alongside the barbell work.

  4. General strength training

    Getting stronger without competing. Open gym access to the same racks, bars and floor, on your own program or one a coach writes.

Not sure which of these you are? Book a Free Facility Tour and walk the floor before you decide.
