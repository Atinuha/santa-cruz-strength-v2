Homepage copy deck

Direct response pass on the Santa Cruz Strength homepage. Every replacement below is ready to paste. Nothing here invents a fact the site cannot support.

Audience: solution aware. They already know they want a gym and are choosing between this one, a chain, and a studio. So the copy differentiates rather than educates. No brand voice profile exists at ./brand/, so voice is derived from the site's own ledger design thesis and from the published member reviews.

The binding constraint. Every factual claim on this site survived an evidence audit, and that audit found several earlier claims to be false. src/config/index.js is the single source, and it carries more than it looks like it does: the address, the phone, the hours, the equipment list, and MEMBERSHIP_TIERS, nine real tiers with prices and terms, under a comment reading "these are commercial representations, not marketing copy". What is genuinely absent is parking, member count, year founded, the busiest hour, and whether a tour needs booking ahead. Where a persuasion move needed one of those, the copy carries a [FACT NEEDED] slot instead of a guess. Those slots are blockers on Mike, not on engineering. Read the config before deciding a fact is missing; see both corrections below.

Tell us what you are training for. It takes about a minute, and it means the person who calls you already knows what you are asking about instead of starting from scratch.

Facts assumed

5. Kept as written

The brief was to rewrite only where a specific gain can be named. These earned their place.

  • "Come see the room for yourself." The closing line. Honest, unhurried, and it matches the ledger thesis the whole design is built on. Nothing generic would beat it.
  • "Book a free tour." Already benefit shaped rather than a command, and every analytics event on the page is named around it. Changing it would cost more than it returns.
  • The three member testimonials. Real published reviews. Rewording them would be fabrication.
  • "See the room, ask questions and decide without pressure." The hero dateline. It is doing objection handling above the fold already.

7. What to test first

  1. Hero headline, specificity against the current sentiment line. The headline carries most of the work, and these two are as far apart in approach as the evidence allows. Measure scroll past the fold and tour form starts.
  2. The new intimidation FAQ, present against absent. If it moves tour requests, that tells you the real blocker is fear rather than price, which changes what to fix next.
  3. Tour form introduction. Smallest change, measured on form completion rate between step one and submit.

None of these can run until analytics is live and a lead can actually be answered. Twilio is rejected at the carrier and email dispatch is gated off, so today a submitted form reaches a database and nothing else.
