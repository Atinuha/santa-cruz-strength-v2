1Problem and Desired End State

Three codebases hold disjoint halves of one product. The destination repository owns the project's identity and history but the weakest implementation: a single-file backend, no tests, no runtime safety, and startup behaviour that resets a production password and launches five outbound schedulers unconditionally. A second repository holds the only written blog bodies. A third holds every reliability guarantee the product needs and the only tests that prove them. None of the three is shippable alone.

End state

The destination repository must become the single system: it keeps its identity, its history and its remote, and receives the proven implementation from the hardened donor plus the written content from the content donor. When convergence is done, all of the following are true locally:

  • No runtime path can reach a live provider, a production database or a real customer without a deliberate, explicitly enabled configuration change.
  • Every public claim traces to a verified source, and no fabricated rating, ranking or count survives anywhere in the tree.
  • Every image is a real photograph of this gym, its real people or its real events, served from the repository rather than a vendor CDN.
  • The lead acquisition path works end to end against local fakes: capture, validation, consent, persistence, queued delivery, observable outcome.
  • The regression suite that currently protects the hardened donor protects the destination instead.

The business objective behind it is unchanged: a local gym site capable of supporting roughly 25 qualified member leads per month, where the owner can defend every word on it.

Source precedence

When two artifacts disagree, this order decides. It exists because the project's original failure was ambiguity about which artifact was authoritative.

  1. Prices, terms, tiers, fees, cancellation: the live public site, mirrored into configuration. Never a mockup, never a rebuild.
  2. Hours: a single configuration source. No page states hours independently. Owner confirmation still pending.
  3. Testimonials and named people: reviews as published, quoted verbatim with attribution. Never an unattributed quote.
  4. Photography: documentary only. A slot with no honest photograph renders nothing rather than substituting a different subject.
  5. Membership transaction: the external hosted provider. Derived from one configuration constant so a club or CRM change is a single edit.
  6. Brand colour: green, corroborated by the live site, the merchandise, and the painted stripe visible in the facility photographs.
  7. Layout and structure only: the approved mockups. Every word of copy on them is placeholder. The owner approved direction, not content.
  8. Indexable routes and canonicals: the route registry, enforced by an automated validator.

5Architecture and Data Contracts

Shape

A single-page public application talks to an HTTP API, which persists to a document store and enqueues work into a durable outbox. A worker claims outbox jobs and dispatches to external providers. Nothing calls a provider inline on the request path. The membership transaction sits entirely outside this system.

Lead contract

One canonical builder produces the lead payload for every consumer form, and a separate builder serves the business enquiry form. The consumer payload carries identity, interest, timeline, attribution, a request identifier for idempotency, and a consent object. Consent must be channel split: operational email eligibility and operational SMS eligibility are distinct fields, and marketing eligibility is distinct again from both.

Contract invariant, learned the hard way. A field the frontend omits becomes a false default at the backend, and a fail-closed gate downstream then suppresses delivery permanently and silently. Every consent field the dispatcher reads must be explicitly present in the payload the form sends. Tests must assert presence, not merely correctness of the fields that happen to be sent.

Lifecycle

Lead lifecycle state is tracked separately from human contact state. Lifecycle answers "what stage is this lead at". Contact state answers "has a person actually been reached", across phone, SMS, email and in person, with an outcome. Transitions are governed by an explicit allowed-transition map rather than free assignment. Collapsing these two into one field is prohibited, because it produces the classic failure where a lead sits in "contacted" after one unanswered voicemail.

Outbox

Five delivery states: pending, processing, retry scheduled, succeeded, terminal failed. Required properties:

  • Atomic claim. A single conditional update carries every guard in its filter. No read-then-write.
  • Reservation token, not worker identity, is the lock. Colliding worker identities across processes cannot double commit.
  • Bounded retry with exponential backoff and a ceiling.
  • Quarantine on unknown outcome. If a provider call began and the lease expired, nobody can prove whether the provider accepted it. That job is quarantined, never auto-retried.
  • Operator replay is gated. Replaying a quarantined job requires explicit confirmation, because the SMS provider accepts no idempotency key and a blind replay delivers a second real message. Ordinary terminal failures replay in one step.
  • Error redaction before persistence, so provider errors carrying personal data are not stored raw.
  • Retention. No policy exists today. Succeeded and terminal jobs accumulate without bound. Deferred, but must be recorded rather than forgotten.

Known limitation to preserve honestly: lease expiry is compared against the worker host's clock rather than a single authoritative clock. Inert for a single worker. With two workers on skewed clocks it is the one remaining path to a double delivery. Either derive lease comparisons from one authoritative clock or document and enforce single-worker operation.

Provider dispatch

Dispatch re-reads the live lead record after claiming the job and before building the message. Consent is evaluated at that moment, not at enqueue time. A suppressed lead terminates non-retryably rather than sending. Quiet hours defer rather than fail. A recipient allowlist prevents a non-production environment from contacting a real member. Email dispatch sends a provider-side idempotency key; SMS dispatch cannot, and the replay gate exists to compensate.

6Local Safety Invariants

These must hold continuously throughout development, not merely at the end. Safety is binary: a path is safe only when its external effects are demonstrably local, faked or disabled.

SI-1. No live provider write. No SMS, email, webhook emission, analytics event or CRM record reaches a real destination from a development run. A test that requires a real message to pass is a failed test design.
SI-2. Persistence is local. The datastore target is a local instance with a throwaway database name. Production connection strings are never copied in for convenience.
SI-3. Default deny. Every outbound capability is governed by an explicit enable flag that defaults to disabled. Absence of a key is not a safety mechanism, because supplying a key must not be sufficient to send.
SI-4. No unconditional startup mutation. Startup must not reset credentials, overwrite existing users or launch recurring jobs without an explicit enable. Index creation is permitted.
SI-5. Inbound is authenticated. Webhook endpoints verify provider signatures and fail closed when verification is unavailable. Token-minting endpoints require authorisation.
SI-6. The remote is protected. The destination's push target is set to a non-pushable value, so an accidental push fails loudly rather than succeeding.
SI-7. Re-verify on change. A prior safety result stays valid only while the relevant configuration and code path are unchanged. Any work touching a provider path or startup re-establishes the invariant before running.

Current state of these invariants is tracked in the accompanying local safety record, which is operational state rather than specification and is updated as work lands.

The rust accent specified by the approved mockups and their direction file appears nowhere in any codebase and is not adopted. 8Search and Content Contract

Mechanism

A route registry declares every route with an indexable flag and a canonical URL. A runtime component resolves the current path against that registry and sets title, description, canonical and robots directives, removing the canonical entirely on non-indexable routes. Unknown paths, staff paths and token paths fall through to no-index, no-follow. The sitemap is generated from the same registry at build time and the generator fails the build if a published article is missing from the registry, so the two cannot drift. Structured data is injected on the home route only, so exactly one business entity appears per crawl. An automated validator enforces the whole contract and fails the build, including a check that no en dash or em dash character appears in shipped copy.

The blog corpus

Two disjoint corpora, zero overlap. The engineering donor declares seven article slugs as published, places them in its route registry and its sitemap, and holds no bodies for any of them. The content donor holds ten fully written article bodies that appear in no registry, no sitemap and no route. Near-miss slug pairs show what happened: articles were renamed and the registry drifted off the content.

Resolution: adopt the ten written bodies, register them, and retire the seven bodyless slugs. Until an article has a body, it must not appear in the registry as indexable, because a sitemap that advertises a soft not-found is worse than an absent page. Existing articles that target real local intent are preserved; no new content is commissioned in this release. Content atomisation and repurposing are deferred until canonical content is approved.

Claim discipline

No numeric or superlative claim appears in public copy without a source in the claims registry. The fabricated rating and ranking claims that survive in the destination's community page are removed and must not return. Public copy contains zero em dashes and zero en dashes, enforced mechanically rather than by review. Membership conversion rate and acquisition cost are unmeasurable from site data and may not be asserted.

Observed externally, not through internals

  • A submitted form produces a persisted record before any success state is shown.
  • A revoked lead receives nothing from a job already queued.
  • A duplicate submission produces no duplicate outbound message.
  • A forged webhook is rejected.

Known gap

Test discovery requires naming modules individually because the test package lacks an initialiser. One small fix makes a single command run everything, which matters more after convergence than before.

10GymMaster Readiness

Verified capability: none. The intended CRM appears in this project as placeholder text on one preview page and one word inside a code comment listing providers the outbox is generic over. There is no client, no environment variable, no field mapping and no test. The migration has been discussed as underway. It has not started.

That is not a criticism of sequencing. Making the outbox provider-generic first is correct, and it means adding this integration later is a matter of writing one dispatch adapter behind an existing boundary. But the plan must stop describing the migration as in progress.

What this release delivers

  • A disabled adapter boundary only. The outbox already treats delivery targets generically; the CRM becomes one more channel behind the same claim, lease, retry and quarantine machinery.
  • A fake client that records intended writes locally and performs none, so the mapping can be exercised without credentials.
  • Duplicate prevention expressed as an idempotency requirement on prospect creation, since a retried job must never create a second prospect.
  • An explicit ownership rule for the dual-running period: which system owns the member record while both are live.

Exact blockers, all external

  • API credentials and a sandbox environment.
  • The prospect and member field mapping, confirmed against the vendor's actual schema rather than assumed.
  • Whether prospect creation is idempotent on the vendor side, and on what key.
  • The migration direction and cutover date relative to the incumbent membership provider.

No vendor behaviour is assumed in this specification. Anything stated about the CRM without a cited official source is a blocker, not a design.

11Out of Scope

  • Pushing, opening a pull request, merging remotely, deploying or activating anything.
  • Any production mutation, including the production datastore and production analytics.
  • Billing, payment handling and any destructive CRM operation.
  • Enabling live providers. Every provider stays disabled or faked.
  • The CRM integration beyond a disabled boundary.
  • New article commissioning, content atomisation and repurposing.
  • Outbox retention policy. Recorded, deferred, inert at current volume.
  • Token-scale consolidation of the two green scales used by the public site and the staff console.
  • Framework and build-tooling modernisation beyond removing the vendor dependency.
  • The second brand. Deferred until this one passes its gates.

14Execution Brief

The implementation must turn the destination repository from a repository that owns the project's identity into one that also owns its proven behaviour, without ever letting a development run touch a real customer. It begins by making the runtime safe, because eleven of twelve outbound surfaces are unsafe in application code rather than configuration and no environment arrangement can compensate: the startup credential reset is removed, the recurring jobs come under an explicit disabled-by-default gate, inbound webhooks gain signature verification that fails closed, and the remote is made non-pushable. Only then does the proven implementation move across, near-total rather than selectively, so that the reliability guarantees and the sixty-eight tests that demonstrate them arrive together rather than as fragments a hand-merge might drop. With that floor in place the product work is narrow and behavioural: vendor the seven real photographs into the repository and fill the empty slots that were wrongly believed unfillable, adopt the ten written articles and retire the seven that were never written, remove the fabricated rating and ranking claims, and bring the nine published membership tiers and their handoff under a single derived source. Each slice should carry one user-visible or system-visible behaviour from input to observable outcome, leave the tree green, and be verified in a real browser where it touches the public experience and against local fakes where it touches a provider. The work is finished not when it compiles but when a run with real credentials present still sends nothing, every sitemap URL returns real content, no image is fetched from a vendor host, and every claim on the site can be defended by the person whose name is on the door.
